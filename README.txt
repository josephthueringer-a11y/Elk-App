ELK SPOT PLANNER — iPhone app
================================

What's in this folder
  index.html            the app
  manifest.json, sw.js  make it installable and work offline
  *.png                 home-screen icons

STEP 1 — Put it online (free, about 2 minutes, do this on a computer)
  1. Unzip this file.
  2. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and make a free account.
  3. Drag the whole "elk-app" folder onto the page.
  4. Netlify gives you a link like https://something.netlify.app
     (You can rename it under Site settings.)

STEP 2 — Install it on your iPhone
  1. Open that link in SAFARI on your iPhone (not Chrome).
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  3. Tap "Add to Home Screen", then "Add".
  4. Open "Elk Spots" from your home screen. It runs full screen like an app.

STEP 3 — Before you leave cell service
  Open the app once while you have signal. After that it works with
  no service: the map, plant info and GPS all keep working.

Using GPS
  Tap the ◎ button. The first time, iPhone asks to share your location. Tap Allow.
  If it never asks: Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services >
  Safari Websites > "While Using".
  GPS works without cell service. Satellite and Directions buttons
  open Google Maps, which needs signal unless you downloaded offline maps there.

Notes
  Plant odds are modeled from habitat, not field counts. The national forest
  boundary is approximate and the forest has private inholdings. Confirm
  ownership in onX before you hunt.
