Plant odds are modeled from habitat (fireweed in cuts and burns, dandelion on roads, landings and pastures, sticky geranium on dry open slopes, false Solomon's seal in moist cedar bottoms), not field counts. By late October most of these are cured, but elk still feed in the same openings and bed in the timber beside them. The unit line and the national forest area (Colville National Forest and the Salmo-Priest Wilderness) are approximate, and the forest has private inholdings. Turn on onX's land ownership layer to confirm the exact line, and check gates, road closures and WDFW rules before you go.
