What the report uses

Terrain from USGS 3DEP elevation (via AWS terrain tiles): elevation, slope, the way the slope faces, and whether it's a ridge, bench, saddle or draw. Openness from USGS aerial photos. Burns from the NIFC interagency fire history. Logging from Forest Service timber harvest records. Land owner from Forest Service ownership data. Sightings from iNaturalist. Plant odds combine these with where each plant grows; they are estimates, not counts. Burns, logging, owner and sightings need signal; terrain and photos work offline once downloaded. Confirm ownership in onX or Gaia before you hunt.
